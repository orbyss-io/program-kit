not an additional validation checklist. Maintained procedures and the selected
checkout's actual instructions govern execution.

over-budget results and finite proof scopes. The separate skill request authorized

The human's skill-design interview accepted proportional section boundaries and
independent safeguard controls, then confirmed the review experience and bounded
forward exercises. These are agreed workflow choices. The F1–F8 sequence does not
prove that every contributor task benefits from its full ceremony or that this new
skill improves arbitrary agent behavior.

Suggested future refinement must be supported by actual use: keep the request,
what was tried, inspectable output, friction/failures, agreed adjustment and next
experiment in the existing handoff. Do not automatically accumulate requirements
or silently edit the skill based on one historical incident.

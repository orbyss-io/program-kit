# A better way to run our neighbourhood repair desk

We run a small volunteer repair desk for people who would rather repair a broken
household item than throw it away. At the moment we use paper notes and messages.
Notes get lost, volunteers repeat questions, and people call because they do not
know what is happening with their item.

I would like a simple application that helps us follow an item from the first
request through deciding whether we can help, arranging the work, and returning
it to its owner. Volunteers need to understand what needs attention and what was
agreed. People bringing items should get a clear acknowledgement and be able to
find out what happens next. Eventually I would like to understand what we repair
most often and where requests get stuck.

We cannot introduce everything at once. I want to start with something useful
enough to try at the desk while we still handle the rest of the process manually,
then improve it in manageable steps. Please help me work out what that first
version should do and how the rest could follow. I have not decided the screens,
the detailed rules, or the technical design.

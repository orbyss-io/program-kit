# Operator notes — keep outside the consumer workspace

Play the product owner, not an architect trying to lead the agent to the answer.
Only PROJECT_REQUEST.md is initial consumer input. Do not paste this file, the
review rubric, the previous trial, or generated test expectations into intake.

Start with the vision as written. Let the installed Program Kit interview decide
which questions are necessary and propose its decomposition. There is no secret
correct framework, folder structure, module count, slice ID or exact slice count.
Do not request named engineering patterns merely because we want to assess whether
Program Kit applies its existing knowledge. Judge its recommendations critically;
do not automatically accept an unsupported recommendation.

When relevant questions arise, these are the scenario's ordinary business facts:

- This is one neighbourhood desk, with a small rotating group of volunteers and
  tens of items in a typical month. It is not a marketplace or a repair franchise.
- A person may ask about a broken item before bringing it in. The desk sometimes
  cannot help. Taking a request does not promise a repair or a completion date.
- Today volunteers acknowledge requests and arrange next steps through ordinary
  conversation or messages. That manual process can continue around an initial
  useful increment. Whether an acknowledgement needs automated delivery is open.
- The owner needs to know which item a conversation concerns. What information to
  collect, who enters it, who may see it, and how to correct a mistake need discussion.
- There is no existing application or migration contract. The sponsor has not chosen
  a stack, database, hosting design, API shape or authentication model.
- This exercise uses fictional people and items. Describe intended real use honestly;
  let the agent distinguish an isolated trial from later deployment. Do not assert
  that real personal data is public or force the old anonymous profile by habit.

Reveal relevant facts when asked; do not front-load the list. For an undecided detail,
say it is undecided and ask for a proportionate recommendation. Record newly chosen
facts and their reasons in the actual interview. Do not silently invent requirements
to make the result fit a hidden test. Do not veto reasonable alternatives because
they differ from the old Camera Reservation app.

Before confirming intake, assess the synthesis as a real customer would: can you
understand what it promises, what it postpones, and what you will be able to try
first? If it proposes the entire application as the first increment, ask why that
is necessary and whether a smaller usable outcome exists. Preserve the uncorrected
proposal and this intervention as trial evidence; a successful coached correction
does not prove unassisted decomposition. Do not approve an unsuitable plan just
to keep the test moving. Do not impose artificial question-count targets.

Never fabricate approval, probe results, costs, or a completed phase. Follow the
existing native review gates. Keep product decisions distinct from model-run
authorization; preparing this scenario authorizes no paid session.

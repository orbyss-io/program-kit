## Program Kit operation review

Retain native analysis and its STRICTLY READ-ONLY boundary. Compare spec, plan and tasks for coherent
user outcomes, real dependencies, applicable security/ownership/provider constraints and risk-selected
tests. Keep minimal enabling work with its first consumer. Do not impose technical-layer phases or
make independent work wait for unrelated future infrastructure.

Review consequential ownership, result/failure, configured-limit, cancellation, cleanup, concurrency
and recovery choices where applicable. Name concrete gaps and affected work; table shape or rule IDs
do not establish decision meaning. A custom stack or local pattern needs suitable contracts and tests,
not consumer qualification in Program Kit's curated catalog.

Confirm that agreed human review pauses and independent walkthroughs are visible in ordinary notes.
Checked tasks and successful engineering checks never manufacture acceptance. Report unresolved
findings without changing source or drafting approval records.

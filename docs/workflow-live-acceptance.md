# Retired paid bootstrap workflow cases

The `workflow-fresh`, `workflow-failure`, `workflow-resume` and `bootstrap-checkpoint`
paid phases are retired. Their issuers, runners and provisioning service stop before
reading or consuming authorization, creating workspaces, installing packages or
starting workers. An old unconsumed manifest cannot authorize another operation.

Use `$speckit-program-kit-governance-product-intake` in the installed consumer to
research and review the founding baseline, then continue with specification intake.
There is no replacement paid founding trial or automatic acceptance claim.

Preserve existing fixtures, schemas, manifests, logs, checkpoints and failed-run
history unchanged. They describe the source and execution they originally bound;
retirement does not convert them into current product acceptance. The historical
workflow-checkpoint reader remains read-only and grants no resume authority.

Independent feature, building-block and upgrade diagnostics retain their one-use,
checkpoint-bound authorization rules; see [live acceptance](live-bootstrap-acceptance.md).
They cannot silently create a substitute bootstrap checkpoint or manufacture a
parent for an existing phase. Provider operation accounting remains separate.
